How it works

Three steps. No forms to fill in.

Your organisation runs a private room and shares one code. That code is the only thing standing between you and someone who will listen.

01

Enter the room code

Use the code your school, church or team shared. This device remembers it, so you only do this once.

02

Pick your person

The directory shows every leader and counsellor with their role and a short bio. Choose who feels right for you.

03

Talk privately

You appear as a random alias, never your name. Reply when you want, close the thread when you're done.
