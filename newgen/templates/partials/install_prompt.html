Keep anylink one tap away

Add anylink to your home screen. It opens like an app — no address bar, no app store, and it stays on this device only.

  1. 1 Tap the Share button in your browser.
  2. 2 Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. 3 Tap Add. anylink appears with your apps.

You can remove it at any time by holding the icon and choosing Remove.
