Privacy

Privacy, in plain language.

anylink exists so someone can ask for help without handing over who they are. This page explains exactly what that means in practice — what is stored, who can see it, and what you can remove.

What we never collect

You do not create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, date of birth or a password. We do not store your IP address, browser fingerprint or location, and there are no advertising or analytics trackers on these pages.

Because there is no account, there is also no “forgot password” and no profile to complete. The only thing that identifies your device is a random token kept in a cookie (see cookies).

Getting into a room

Each room has a random identifier in its link and a short numeric code — four or six digits — that the organisation gives you. The link finds the room; the code proves you were invited. Both are needed.

Once a code is accepted, this browser can open that room's leader directory for 12 hours. After that you enter the code again. Your organisation can change the code at any time, which immediately stops old codes from working.

What is stored

  • An alias such as “Quiet Lion 42”, generated for you. It is not derived from anything about you.
  • Your messages in that room, with the time they were sent and whether they were delivered and read.
  • A hashed device token so this browser can find its conversations again. The raw token stays in your cookie; the database only holds a one-way hash.
  • A safety flag if a message suggests a risk of harm. It marks the conversation for the leaders; it does not identify you.

We do not store your name, contacts, photos, files or precise location. Messages you write are visible to the person you chose to talk to.

Who can read your conversation

Only the leader or counsellor you picked can read the thread. If they are unavailable, they may transfer it to another leader in the same room; the conversation shows a short system note when that happens.

People who administer the room can add or remove leaders, see the room code and pause the room, but they do not automatically get access to conversations. No conversation is ever listed publicly.

As with any conversation, the person on the other side could repeat what you say. anylink cannot control that, so share only what you are comfortable sharing.

Cookies on this device

  • Session cookie — keeps you signed in as a leader, or remembers that you entered a room code. It expires after 3 days, or when you sign out.
  • Device token cookie — a random, HTTP-only value that links this browser to your alias and conversations. It can last up to a year.
  • No third-party cookies, pixels or tracking scripts.

Clearing cookies removes the device token. The conversations still exist for the leaders, but this browser can no longer find them, and we cannot recover that link for you.

How long it stays

A conversation stays until you delete it from the chat menu, or until the organisation removes it. Deleting a conversation deletes its messages and your alias from the active database.

A leader may keep a safety report if there was a concern about harm. Reports keep the alias and the reason, not your identity, because we do not have it. Room codes and access records are kept only as long as the room exists.

Safety and emergencies

anylink is not an emergency service and rooms are not monitored around the clock. If you are in immediate danger, call 112 (emergency), 191 (police) or 193 (ambulance).

Messages that suggest a risk of harm are flagged for the leaders in that room so they can respond sooner. The flag is visible to them; it is not shared with anyone outside the room.

Your choices

  • Delete the thread from the conversation menu whenever you want.
  • Leave the room by clearing this site's cookies or letting the 12-hour access window expire.
  • Ask for a new code through the organisation that invited you if the old one has been shared too widely.
  • Say no to any question. You never have to give your name or contact details to use anylink.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Material changes will be reflected here before they apply to new conversations.

Questions about a specific room — who runs it, who its leaders are, or what it keeps — go to the school, church or organisation that shared your invitation. They control the room; anylink provides the software.
